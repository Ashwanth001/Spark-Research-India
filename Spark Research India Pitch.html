The Opportunity

India produces brilliant young scientists — most never hear of IRIS.

The IRIS National Fair is India's premier pre‑college science competition. Top performers earn a place on Team India at ISEF, the world's largest pre‑college STEM stage held annually in the USA.

Yet only a fraction of Indian students ever discover the IRIS → ISEF pathway, let alone receive the structured mentorship needed to compete at this level. That is the gap we exist to close.

The Scholarship Programme

Great science should not depend on a bank balance.

100%
Programme fee covered for selected students

Spark Research India is committed to ensuring that economic background never limits a student's potential. We offer full scholarships covering the entire programme fee — Standard or Advanced — to deserving students from underprivileged communities.

Who Can Apply
Students from government schools, EWS families, SC/ST/OBC communities, or with demonstrated financial need.
What's Covered
100% of the programme fee — all mentorship hours, materials and support, with no hidden costs.
Selection Criteria
Scientific curiosity, a motivation letter, a teacher or school recommendation, and a short interview.
